Consumer Rights

Under CBK regulations, Kenyan citizens are entitled to one free annual credit report directly from licensed bureaus (Metropol, TransUnion, Creditinfo).

Payment Procedure

To access your report and score, please pay KES 50 to Safaricom paybill 212121 with your ID as account number.

ONLY pay using your registered mobile number as provided above.

📜

Clearance Certificate Application

To apply for an official Certificate of Clearance, your credit record must show no non-performing loan accounts.

A processing fee of KES 2,200 applies as per Central Bank of Kenya (CBK) guidelines.
