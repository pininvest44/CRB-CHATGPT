Clearance Certificate Application

To apply for an official Certificate of Clearance, your credit record must show no non-performing loan accounts.

A processing fee of KES 2,200 applies as per Central Bank of Kenya (CBK) guidelines.

Please pull a fresh Credit Report first to ensure all defaulted loans have been fully settled before proceeding.
